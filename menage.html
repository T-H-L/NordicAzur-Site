Services à la personne

Pay only half with the tax credit

NordicAzur Ménage is a declared provider of services à la personne. If your tax household is in France, 50% of what you pay us comes back to you as a tax credit, even if you pay no income tax.

01

We clean

You pay your invoice as usual, by bank transfer.

02

You receive a tax certificate

Every year, before 31 March, we send you your annual tax certificate (attestation fiscale).

03

You get 50% back

Enter the amount in your income tax return and receive 50% back, on up to €12,000 of expenses per year.

%
Example: 2 hours every week You pay €70 per visit. After the tax credit, your real cost is €35 per visit.

Tax credit of 50% of the amounts paid, under article 199 sexdecies of the French General Tax Code (CGI), within the conditions and limits set by law.

Frequently asked questions

Do you bring the cleaning products?

We can bring our own products and equipment, or use yours if you prefer. Just tell us when you book.

Do I need to be at home?

No. Many clients give us a key, which we keep securely. You are welcome to be there for the first visit, so we can agree on everything together.

What if I need to cancel or move a visit?

Just let us know as early as possible, ideally 48 hours before, and we will find a new date with you.

Who can get the tax credit?

Households whose tax home is in France, for cleaning at their main or second home in France. If you live abroad and only own a holiday home here, our property care services may suit you better.

Away for long periods?

NordicAzur also looks after second homes: regular inspections, photo reports and practical local help while you are away.
