Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Which areas do you cover?

We cover Vence and the surrounding villages: Saint-Paul-de-Vence, Tourrettes-sur-Loup, Saint-Jeannet, La Colle-sur-Loup and Cagnes-sur-Mer. For properties a little further away, just ask.

How do I receive my reports?

After each visit you receive a short report with photos by email or WhatsApp, whichever you prefer. If anything needs attention, we contact you straight away.

How are my keys kept safe?

Your keys are stored securely and are only handed to craftsmen or suppliers with your agreement.

What does the free consultation include?

A conversation about your property and your needs, ideally with a visit on site. You then receive a written proposal with a monthly price, with no obligation.

Can I combine a package with extra services?

Yes. All à la carte services can be added to any package when you need them, and are always agreed with you in advance.
